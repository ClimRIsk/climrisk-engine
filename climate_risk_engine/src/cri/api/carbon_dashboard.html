From invoices to an assurance-ready GHG inventory

Download the template, fill in your entities and activity data — fuel and electricity invoices, refrigerant top-ups, travel, waste, supplier data and spend — and upload it. The engine calculates Scope 1, Scope 2 both location-based and market-based, and all 15 Scope 3 categories, consolidated by the boundary you choose.

Every reported number keeps its input, factor, working, evidence reference and data-quality score. The assurance pack (.xlsx) lets an auditor re-perform each line. Scope 3 categories without data must be excluded with a reason — nothing drops out silently.

Factors: UK DESNZ 2026 · US EPA eGRID2023 · Green-e 2025 residual mix · AIB 2025 European residual mix · Ember · World Bank T&D losses · US EPA USEEIO v1.3.
