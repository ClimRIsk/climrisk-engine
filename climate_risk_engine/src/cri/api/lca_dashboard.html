What this calculates

The cradle-to-gate carbon footprint (life-cycle stages A1–A3) of one tonne of product, built from your plant's own data: raw materials and their upstream factors (A1), inbound freight (A2), and at the plant (A3) process CO₂, fuel combustion, the fuels' supply chains (well-to-tank) and lifecycle grid electricity.

Every result shows its uncertainty band (Monte Carlo, width set by data quality), what was excluded and why, biogenic CO₂ reported separately, and a 2025–2050 trajectory as the grid decarbonises under each NGFS Phase 5 scenario. Cement also gets an EU CBAM view.

Inputs that differ widely between plants (clinker ratio, kiln fuel energy, fuel mix, electricity use) are required; the engine does not fill them in for you.
