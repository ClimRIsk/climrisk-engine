## Known limitations left as they are (documented, not advertised)
| Item | Note |
|---|---|
| Portfolio loss model (`/portfolio/risk`) | Sector × region model; company physical/transition scores do not change EAL/VaR (schema now says so). Asset-level risk is in the Risk Analyst |
| Legacy CRI rating / company engine | Still internally inconsistent for many companies (e.g. rating A with a large NZE EV loss; negative EVs). Kept for the app's existing assessment flow and labelled `model_status: legacy`; the Risk Analyst is the recommended path. Recommendation: rebuild the app's assessment flow on the Risk Analyst |
| Implied temperature rise (`cri.climate.paris_alignment`) | Sector budget shares far too small — rated 12 %/yr reducers above 2 °C. Withheld from `/agent/assess`; still called by the batch job runner (`agent/job_runner.py`) — needs recalibration before any client use |
| `data_acquisition/eutl_client.py` | Calls an EUTL API host that does not resolve (like the old Aqueduct "live API"); fails silently. Verified emissions must come from the client or the Union Registry downloads |
| Public widget on Render | Needs the Render service redeployed with this engine version; until then the homepage widget cannot reach `/public/company-risk` |
| Flood skill | Area model shows moderate skill in 3 US counties; no evidence yet outside the US |
| Abatement and earnings layers | Lever costs are published ranges (some analyst judgement, flagged per lever), flat in real terms, mixing source years; decision price is net of pass-through (conservative — no windfall credit); myopic operator; no financing or permitting limits; levelised costs charged only to 2050. Earnings: financials must match the emissions perimeter; rating/PD are a coverage-based proxy; no refinancing or waivers; impairment without terminal value (increment vs baseline only). Plant life: design life by sector is analyst judgement; legal phase-out dates are user-supplied (`retirement_year`), no national table is embedded (unverified). CBAM: EU price = max(today's ETS price, NGFS EU path), no timing lag, share of exports supplied by the user. Not yet modelled: demand elasticity beyond NGFS sector output, business interruption |
| Physical Risk Report (sector-wise, no financials) | Reports mixed physical and financial output | 🔧 `cri.physical_report`: per site — how it sits (elevation, terrain, land cover, distance to water, catchment), hazard zones (JRC river, Deltares coastal at 2050, FLOPROS defences, IBTrACS cyclone, slope, wildfire, heat indicator, WRI Aqueduct water stress), construction year → age, design life, ageing notes, sector-specific consequences and measures; portfolio grouped by sector; HTML/PDF/JSON via `/reports/physical-risk[.html|.pdf]`, workbench `/reports/physical-risk/dashboard`. No monetary figures (tested). Ratings are hazard-intensity thresholds, not damage; heat is a land-cover indicator only |

## Asset Twin, resident agent and story reports (7 Oct 2026)
| Claim (what may be said) | What backs it | Limits that must travel with it |
|---|---|---|
| "3D model of a real site from open footprints and terrain" | `cri.asset_twin`: OpenStreetMap footprints (mirror fallback) + AWS Terrarium terrain ~9 m; where OSM has none an illustrative layout is shown and flagged ILLUSTRATIVE | Heights, equipment levels and design winds are assumed by structure kind until edited in the viewer |
| "Flood replay is a hydraulic simulation" | `cri.hydraulics`: 2D local-inertial shallow-water solver (Bates 2010), buildings as obstructions, mass-conservation checked (tests: still water, wall blocks, crest overtopping, barrier protects), forced with the JRC/Deltares map level | The map is the ceiling for the level; duration assumed (1.5 h rise, hold to 3 h); no rainfall, drains, pumps or waves; solver can fill low pockets a 90 m map smooths over — flagged in the finding and in the report |
| "All-cause wind, not only cyclones" | `cri.wind_extreme`: ERA5 daily gusts 1950+ via Open-Meteo, Gumbel (L-moments), 10/50/100/250-yr levels (100/250 flagged extrapolated) | ERA5 smooths peaks so values are floors; tornado and downburst not modelled |
| "Protection is verified, not asserted" | Raise-equipment, plinth, barrier and wind-retrofit measures re-evaluated; a flood wall is verified by re-running the solver with the wall in the model | Sized to the modelled water level or gust plus stated freeboard; no costs are invented |
| "Scenario lab ranks solutions" | `resident.scenario`: scenario matrix × water-level shifts / wind scales, solutions ranked by physical performance and robustness margin | Flood margins shift the solver depth field (screening-grade); wind margins exact for the power-law model |
| "An AI agent inside the engine that needs no external software" | `cri.resident`: intent model trained in-engine (numpy TF-IDF + softmax); held-out-phrasing accuracy published in `/resident/status` (≈63% model-only, ≈71% with keyword cues on never-seen templates; improves with user corrections via `/resident/retrain`); memory in SQLite; replies composed from tool results by templates | It is a trained intent classifier + planner, not a general language model; low-confidence requests are asked back, not guessed. Geocoding a place name and the live research feeds use public internet services; coordinates and registered assets work offline |
| "Researches live hazards and drafts LinkedIn posts" | `resident.research`: GDACS (TC, flood), USGS (M6+), NOAA CPC ONI → mapped onto the asset register → story → post + image card | Event footprints are centroid + radius (TC 100–150 km, flood 75 km): "assets in the vicinity", never "damaged". Publishing needs `LINKEDIN_ACCESS_TOKEN` + `LINKEDIN_AUTHOR_URN` in `.env` and an explicit "Approve & publish" click; chat text alone never posts (tested with mocked LinkedIn API) |
| "Separate customised story reports for physical, transition, financial, LCA, ISO 14067, carbon audit and CBAM" | `cri.story_reports` + `/story-reports/*`: one story format (hook, dated evidence, asset-level story, per-reader implications, method and limits, numbered sources); sector template, section picker, client branding; HTML/PDF/Markdown/JSON; numbers trace to engine results (tests assert this) | Physical report takes ~1–2 min (builds the twin + solver); transition/financial use screening-grade asset values unless supplied |
